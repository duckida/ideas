# Commit regularly

Commit your work as you go — don't save it all up for one giant commit at the end. Keep commit messages short and descriptive (a concise summary line, e.g. "add search button to ideas page"). Committing in small steps keeps the history readable, makes review easier, and gives you safe restore points if something breaks.
